Hotel Guestroom FF&E

From casegoods and beds to wardrobes and headboard panels — we source, coordinate, and deliver complete guestroom FF&E packages for hotels of every scale, from boutique properties to large-scale resorts.

Scope of Work

  • Casegoods: nightstands, desks, dressers, minibar cabinets, luggage racks
  • Beds: headboards, bed frames, under-pannels, roll-out trundle beds
  • Wardrobes & closets: built-in and freestanding options with custom shelving
  • Desking & workstation units: integrated writing desks with power/data solutions
  • Luggage racks, valet stands, and in-room accessories
  • Headboard panels: upholstered, wood-veneer, and acoustic panels
  • Mattress sourcing coordination (paired with bedding package)
  • Drapery & curtain track systems for guestrooms

Project Categories

  • 5-Star & Luxury Hotels
  • Upper-Upscale Select Service
  • Extended Stay & Serviced Apartments
  • Boutique & Lifestyle Hotels
  • Resort Villas & Condo-Hotels

Why Choose Us

  • Access to 19+ specialized Foshan factories covering every guestroom category
  • Single PO covers all guestroom FF&E — one price, one accountability chain
  • Fire compliance: CAL 117 (US), BS 7177 (UK/Middle East), UL listed
  • Climate-adapted material specs: moisture-resistant for tropical, anti-cracking for dry climates
  • Factory-direct pricing with 30–45% cost advantage vs. Western sourcing
  • Dedicated QC team on-the-ground in Foshan for every production batch

Certifications & Standards

  • CAL 117 — Flammability (US)
  • BS 7177 — Fire resistance for upholstery (UK/Middle East)
  • CARB Phase 2 — Formaldehyde emission (US)
  • BIFMA — Commercial furniture safety
  • ISO 9001 — Factory quality management
