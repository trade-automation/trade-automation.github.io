Hotel FF&E Services

Eight production and project disciplines, coordinated under one supplier of record. Casegoods, soft seating, lobby and public-area furniture, lighting, drapery, mattress and bath vanities are quoted, produced, inspected and consolidated on a single purchase order — from shop drawings to loaded container.

What does foshanFFE.com supply?

foshanFFE.com is a hospitality FF&E integrator, not a trading company. The scope covers complete guestroom packages plus public areas, backed by 19 partner factories in the Foshan production cluster. Typical project budgets run $4,500–$8,000 per key for 4-star properties and $9,000–$16,000 per key for 5-star properties, with casegoods-only scopes starting around $1,800–$3,200 per room. Standard production is 45–75 days, plus 25–30 days door-to-port to Jebel Ali.

Why buyers consolidate with one integrator

  • One PO, one invoice, one shipping schedule — instead of eight suppliers, eight deposits and eight sets of documents
  • One specification standard — the same finish, foam density and hardware spec across guestroom, lobby and F&B
  • One inspection regime — in-line and pre-shipment QC across every factory before anything is loaded
  • One consolidation point — multi-factory cargo merged into a single container program to cut freight and demurrage
  • One accountable party — when a container lands short or damaged, there is one supplier of record to resolve it
