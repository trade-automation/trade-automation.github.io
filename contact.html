What Happens After You Send the Form

Every enquiry goes to a named account manager rather than a shared inbox. Four steps, and the same person stays on the file.

  1. 1

    Acknowledged within one business day

    You get a reply from someone who has read the form, not an auto-responder. If the enquiry lands on a Friday evening in China, expect it Monday morning.

  2. 2

    Scope confirmed before pricing

    We ask about room count, star rating, opening date and destination port. Anything that affects lead time or certification gets flagged at this stage, not after the PO.

  3. 3

    Itemised pricing

    A bill of quantities with unit prices per item, packing and freight broken out separately. We confirm receipt first, then quote once the scope is complete.

  4. 4

    Samples before the production line starts

    Where a finish, fabric or joint detail matters, we build a sample piece and ship it for approval. Nothing goes into bulk until you sign off on the physical item.

Certification and Compliance

Name the destination country in your message. It changes what we specify, not just what we document.

United States

UL listed components, CARB Phase 2 and TSCA Title VI board, California TB 117-2013 upholstery.

Middle East

SASO and ESMA conformity, GCC lighting white-list components where the project specifies them.

European Union

EN 13501 fire reaction classes, EN 16798-2 ventilation and automation interfaces.

Australia and New Zealand

AS/NZS 4680 framing, AFRDI-tested commercial seating.

If a project needs a test report we do not already hold, we commission it at a third-party lab and send the certificate number before shipment. Say so early, because some labs run four to six weeks.
