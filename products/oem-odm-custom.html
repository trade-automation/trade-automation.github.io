OEM Service: Your Design, Our Manufacturing

Quality Standards & Certifications

  • Low VOC Hotel Furniture: All production uses CARB P2 / TSCA Title VI compliant substrates, water-based or UV-cured finishes, low-VOC adhesives (≤0.5 mg/m³). Formaldehyde testing per ASTM E1333
  • Fire Safety: Custom fire-retardant treatment to target market standards — BS 5852, CAL TB 117, NFPA 260, EN 1021. Documentation provided with every shipment
  • Structural Testing: BIFMA testing per furniture category. Load testing, cycle testing, stability testing per ANSI/BIFMA standards
  • Environmental: FSC chain-of-custody available, recycled content materials on request, EPD documentation for LEED projects
  • QC Process: Three-stage inspection (in-process, pre-assembly, pre-shipment). Sample approval required before mass production. Third-party inspection available (SGS, TÜV, Bureau Veritas, Intertek)
  • Packaging & Shipping: Custom packaging designed per product. Flat-pack or fully assembled. Container loading plans optimized for shipping efficiency. Photo documentation before sealing

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the difference between OEM and ODM for hotel furniture?

A: OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturing) means you provide the design and we manufacture to your specifications. ODM (Original Design Manufacturing) means we develop the design based on your requirements — saving design costs and sampling time. foshanFFE.com offers both, plus full custom development for projects requiring unique brand identity pieces.

Q: How long does custom hotel furniture sampling take?

A: Standard sampling timeline: 15-21 days for OEM (your drawings), 21-28 days for ODM (our design), 28-45 days for fully custom pieces including structural prototyping. foshanFFE.com provides 3D renderings within 7 days before physical sampling begins, reducing revision cycles.
