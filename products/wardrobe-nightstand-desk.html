  • Dimensions: Fully custom widths (300mm–2400mm), depths (350mm–650mm), and heights (600mm–2400mm). We work from your room layout drawings and coordinate with millwork teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the best material for hotel wardrobes?

A: For 4-star hotels, MDF with HPL (High Pressure Laminate) provides the best cost-to-durability ratio. For 5-star hotels, plywood with natural wood veneer (oak, walnut, or ash) offers premium aesthetics. Luxury properties may use solid wood frames with veneered panels. foshanFFE.com specifies moisture-resistant core materials for bathroom-adjacent wardrobes.

Q: Should hotel nightstands include USB charging?

A: Yes — USB charging ports (USB-A + USB-C) are now expected in 4-star and above hotels. foshanFFE.com integrates charging modules into nightstands, desks, and headboards with concealed cable management. Charging modules are UL/cUL listed for US projects and CE certified for European projects.
