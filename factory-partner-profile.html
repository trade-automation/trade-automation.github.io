Crafting Hotel Furniture Since Day One

Our partner factory is located in Foshan, Guangdong — China's furniture manufacturing capital. The company is committed to providing high-end hotel furniture, premium wood products, and decorative indoor solutions for luxury hospitality projects worldwide.

With advanced production technology imported from Italy and Germany, the factory works exclusively with premium solid wood species including walnut, oak, maple, ash, cherry, mahogany, and teak — each selected for durability, grain beauty, and sustainability compliance.

Environmental protection is built into every production step. The factory conducts per-batch formaldehyde testing for all MDF, plywood, paint, and glue — ensuring every piece meets international emission standards before it leaves the workshop.

From Raw Log to Finished Masterpiece

Six meticulous production steps — each one a checkpoint of quality that separates factory-grade output from furniture-grade craft.

1

Material Selection

Only 100–300 year old logs from natural forests are accepted. Harvesting runs September through December; air-drying takes place at origin. Materials are processed between March and September — ensuring optimal moisture equilibrium before production begins.

2

Wood Shaping

Steam treatment enables precise bending for curved components. Temperature and duration are strictly controlled to prevent fiber breakage or strength loss — critical for decorative elements in hotel lobbies and suites.

3

Drying

Both artificial (steam kiln) and natural (air circulation) drying methods are employed. Moisture content is strictly monitored throughout the cycle — ensuring dimensional stability in any climate, from desert heat to tropical humidity.

4

Hand Carving

Master craftsmen execute detailed sculptural work by hand — decorative panels, headboard motifs, and ornamental moldings that define luxury hotel interiors. Each piece carries the unmistakable character of human artistry.

5

Polishing

A 12-grit progression from coarse to finest sandpaper prepares every surface. The final finish is completed by hand — achieving the silk-smooth texture expected in five-star guestroom furnishings.

6

Lacquering

Natural lacquer (国漆) — known as the "King of Coatings" — is applied by master craftsmen. The result is a finish that is corrosion-resistant, deeply luminous, and ages with a patina that improves over decades of hotel use.

Quality Control for Fixed & Built-in Furniture

Four critical control points that ensure every installed piece meets the brand standard — from factory floor to hotel suite.

Wood Grain Matching

Same veneer batch for entire room sets. Per-order grain matching with zero color tolerance — ensuring visual consistency across every guestroom in a floor or wing.

Flatness Control

2-meter straight-edge tolerance within 2mm. Anti-deformation treatment is applied inside door panels and large flat surfaces — preventing warping in varied humidity conditions.

Crack Prevention

Factory pre-assembled corners with mortise-and-tenon joinery secured by wooden dowels. Moisture content held within controlled range — eliminating the thermal stress cracks that plague poorly built furniture.

Installation Coordination

On-site technical guidance during installation. Our team coordinates directly with renovation contractors for efficient scheduling — reducing downtime and preventing costly rework.
