The Challenge

A Complex Multi-Zone Interior, One Cohesive Vision

A 212-seat restaurant spanning two floors with VIP rooms, specialty areas, and a demanding design brief required meticulous coordination across every discipline:

  • Two-floor venue with complex spatial requirements — commercial kitchen, butcher area, VIP rooms, praying rooms, children's playground, and elevator integration.
  • Unified design language across 212 seats while differentiating VIP zones — the client demanded visual consistency with private-room exclusivity.
  • Tight coordination needed between ceiling treatments, wall paneling, furniture placement, and door positioning — all interdependent.
  • Multiple material families — MDF veneer, sintered stone, marble, ANC fabric, stainless steel — requiring precise finishing consistency throughout.
Our Solution

One Integrator, 19 Partner Factories, Complete Coordination

As a hospitality FF&E integrator based in Foshan — China's furniture manufacturing capital — every aspect of this restaurant interior was managed through a single project structure:

Comprehensive Shop Drawings

Detailed shop drawings for every zone — ground floor, first floor, VIP-1, VIP-2 — with millwork specs per wall (WALL-A through WALL-E).

Unified Design Palette

Emerald green + walnut wood + gold paint palette standardized across all zones for complete visual cohesion.

27 Matching Doors

Custom-designed doors with matching sapele veneer finish, gold handles, and integrated cladding panels for seamless wall transitions.

Coordinated Delivery

Furniture production aligned with interior millwork timelines to ensure single-delivery installation. One PO, one project manager, one quality standard.

Frequently Asked Questions

A restaurant FF&E package covers all interior furnishings and fixtures: custom furniture (tables, chairs, booth seating), ceiling treatments (gypsum, wood veneer, decorative panels), wall treatments (veneered panels, CNC decorative panels, fabric upholstery), doors and door hardware, lighting integration, and specialty area buildouts such as kitchens and playgrounds. Everything is managed under a single purchase order with one integrator overseeing design, production, quality control, and delivery.
Yes. Our team works directly from architectural floor plans and interior design drawings to produce detailed shop drawings for every zone. We coordinate dimensions across millwork, furniture, ceilings, and doors to ensure everything fits precisely. Our engineering team reviews each wall, each opening, and each furniture placement before production begins.
Custom furniture is developed from the client's design intent or reference images. Our in-house design team creates detailed production drawings, selects materials (wood species, stone types, fabric, metal finishes), and produces prototypes for approval. In this project, dining tables featured sintered stone and marble tops with custom metal bases, chairs combined walnut wood shells with velvet upholstery, and booth seating was built to match the exact wall panel dimensions.
All doors and millwork undergo a three-stage quality control process: incoming material inspection, in-line production checks, and pre-shipment full inspection with photo reports. Doors are manufactured to 42mm thickness with consistent veneer application, precision edge-banding, and tested hardware. Materials meet international fire safety standards and low-emission requirements (CARB P2 equivalent). Every component is inspected before container loading.
We offer both supply-only and supply-plus-installation options. For this project, we provided comprehensive shop drawings, coordinated production across all categories, consolidated shipping in full containers, and provided on-site installation guidance. Our integrator model means one team manages the entire scope — from factory floor to venue floor — ensuring consistency in quality and timeline.
