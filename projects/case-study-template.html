The Challenge

Complex Multi-Category Sourcing, One Deadline

The client needed to furnish 120 guest rooms plus lobby, restaurant, and corridor areas for a new boutique hotel opening in Dubai. They faced several critical challenges:

  • Multiple product categories — casegoods, upholstery, lighting, drapery, and lobby furniture — each requiring different manufacturing expertise.
  • Tight 12-week timeline — from final design approval to on-site delivery, with zero tolerance for delays.
  • Strict compliance requirements — all materials needed to meet UAE fire safety codes, UL certification for electrical components, and CARB P2 for formaldehyde emissions.
  • Cost pressure — the client was comparing against Western suppliers and needed competitive pricing without sacrificing quality.
Our Solution

One Partner, 19 Factories, One PO

As a hospitality FF&E integrator based in Foshan — China's furniture manufacturing capital — we coordinated the entire project through our network of 19 specialized partner factories:

Single Purchase Order

All product categories consolidated under one PO — simplified procurement, unified quality standards, one point of accountability.

Rigorous QC Process

Three-stage quality control: incoming material inspection, in-line production checks, and pre-shipment full inspection with photo reports.

Parallel Production

Multiple factories producing simultaneously — casegoods, upholstery, lighting, and drapery all on parallel tracks to meet the 12-week deadline.

Consolidated Shipping

All goods consolidated at our Foshan warehouse, then shipped in full containers to Dubai — reducing shipping costs and ensuring coordinated delivery.
