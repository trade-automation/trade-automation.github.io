Guestroom and Bathroom Details

The photographs move from the main bedroom to the bunk area and two bathroom vanities. The notes below describe only what is visible.

Main bedroom

The two views show the bed setting, blue padded headboard, gold-tone wardrobes and a dressing counter with a mirror.

Custom bunk area

A raised white bunk with a curved gold-tone guardrail, a side ladder and a long counter below.

Bathroom vanities

One vanity has a rectangular illuminated mirror and vessel basin; the other has a round illuminated mirror, an angular basin and a wall-mounted tap.
