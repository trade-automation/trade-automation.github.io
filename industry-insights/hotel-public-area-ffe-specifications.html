Production & Shipping Considerations

Public area FF&E items are significantly larger and more complex than guestroom furniture. Key logistics points from this project:

📦 Segmented Manufacturing

Items exceeding container dimensions are produced in sections:

  • Feature wall (4.6m tall): Split into 2-3 horizontal sections, joined on-site with concealed connecting hardware
  • Wine cabinet (5.3m wide): Segmented into 3-4 vertical modules
  • Bar counter (9.8m arc): Produced in 4-5 curved segments, numbered and mapped for assembly
  • Reception desk: Main body + side return as separate pieces
Critical coordination points: Each segmented item requires detailed assembly drawings, hardware kits, and — for stone-clad pieces — on-site joint treatment instructions. We provide full installation documentation and can arrange remote video support during installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are used for luxury hotel lobby furniture?

Premium hotel lobby FF&E typically combines E1-grade engineered board with natural stone (marble, travertine), 304 stainless steel (brushed or antique finish), brass or bronze metal frames, MDF wave panels with lacquer finish, and integrated LED lighting. Hardware from brands like Hafele ensures durability in high-traffic public areas.

How is a hotel reception desk manufactured and shipped?

Hotel reception desks are produced in modular sections for container shipping. A standard 2800mm desk with side return is built as 2-3 segments: main counter body, side return cabinet, and internal storage. Each section is wrapped individually with foam + plywood crate. Assembly hardware and joinery drawings are included for on-site installation.

What is the difference between guestroom FF&E and public area FF&E?

Guestroom FF&E focuses on standardized, repetitive items (beds, nightstands, wardrobes) produced in volume. Public area FF&E involves highly customized, one-off pieces (reception desks, feature walls, bar counters, decorative screens) with complex materials like natural stone and metal cladding. Public area items require more design coordination, mockup approval, and on-site fitting tolerance.
