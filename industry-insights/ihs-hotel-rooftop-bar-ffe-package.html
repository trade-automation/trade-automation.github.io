Material & Weather Resistance Summary

🌴 Solid Teak Wood Pieces

  • Items: Sun loungers, round day beds, outdoor dining chairs, shower stool
  • Properties: Natural oils provide water & rot resistance; ideal for outdoor use
  • Maintenance: Optional teak oil application to maintain golden color; will weather to silver-grey naturally

🪢 Woven Rope & Rattan Details

  • Items: Lounge chairs, dining chairs, bar stools, side tables
  • Materials: Hand-woven synthetic rope on powder-coated iron or aluminum frames
  • Benefits: UV-resistant, quick-drying, adds texture & visual warmth to rooftop settings

🪨 Marble Top Tables

  • Items: Coffee tables, dining tables, bar tables, high tables
  • Construction: Natural marble slab with 304/305 stainless steel edge banding
  • Bases: Painted plywood, powder-coated iron, or plywood with ash veneer

🌧️ Waterproof & Quick-Dry Systems

  • Cushions: Quick-dry foam core (open-cell structure drains water)
  • Fabric: Customer-supplied, must be pre-treated for waterproofing
  • Frames: Aluminum alloy and powder-coated iron resist corrosion

Project Terms

Payment: 50% T/T after PI confirmed, 50% balance before loading.
