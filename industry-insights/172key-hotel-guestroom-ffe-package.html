Material Strategy

Why Tech Wood Veneer instead of Natural Veneer?
Tech wood veneer (reconstituted veneer) delivers consistent grain pattern and color across 172 rooms — critical for brand consistency. It's also CARB Phase 2 compliant for formaldehyde emission, making it ideal for Middle East and North American hotel projects.

Why One PO Matters

This project required 15 different product categories — from headboards to ottomans, casegoods to upholstery, wood to stone to steel. Under a fragmented model, the hotel developer would manage 8-10 separate suppliers, coordinate multiple production schedules, and handle separate shipping documents.

Under our integration model:

  • One contact — Mr. Chen handles everything
  • One production timeline — all 1,666 pieces ship together
  • One quality standard — unified QC across 19 Foshan partner factories
  • One shipping document — one BL, one customs clearance at destination

That's the difference between managing suppliers and managing your hotel project.
