Why Single-PO Integration Matters

Managing 5,400+ line items across 265 apartments and 2 towers requires one central integrator coordinating production, QC and logistics from a single purchase order. Benefits include:

  • One point of contact – no multi-vendor coordination headaches
  • Consistent quality – unified QC protocol across all categories
  • Optimized container loading – mixed furniture containers planned by room type and floor sequence
  • Single shipping timeline – all items ship together, reducing customs complexity
  • Cost transparency – one invoice, no hidden margins from middlemen
