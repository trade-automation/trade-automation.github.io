Material Summary by Category

🛋️ Indoor Seating (Sofas, Chairs, Stools)

  • Frames: Solid ash wood, powder-coated iron, or plywood core
  • Upholstery: High-density resilient foam with customer-supplied fabric
  • Finishes: Spray paint, natural wood veneer, fabric-wrapped details

🌴 Outdoor & Terrace Furniture

  • Primary Material: Solid teak wood (Tectona grandis) — naturally weather-resistant
  • Alternative: 304 stainless steel with sintered stone tops
  • Cushions: Quick-dry foam with waterproof fabric treatment
  • Details: Hand-woven rope accents on select pieces

🪨 Tables & Surfaces

  • Tops: Natural marble slab, sintered stone
  • Bases: Plywood with figured ash veneer, 304 stainless steel
  • Edge banding: 304 stainless steel trim on marble pieces

🛏️ Beds

  • Frame: Plywood with natural figured ash veneer
  • Slats: Solid pine, finished smooth
  • Height: 295mm platform height for box spring + mattress

Project Terms

Payment: 50% T/T after PI confirmed, 50% balance before loading.

Lead Time: 30–45 days from deposit receipt.

Warranty: 3 years on all furniture, per confirmed production drawings.

Packaging: Standard export packaging included. Wooden crate packaging available at additional cost.

Shipping: EXW Foshan. Ocean freight quoted separately.
