Hotel Guestroom Furniture: One Room Set, Specified Once

A guestroom package is not a furniture list. It is a set of SKUs that must survive a decade of check-outs, pass a brand standard audit, and fit a room drawn before the furniture was chosen. This page shows what the set contains, what changes between room types, and what we need to price it.

The short answer

A hotel guestroom furniture package is the FF&E set inside the room: casegoods (bed base, nightstands, wardrobe, desk, media console), upholstery (headboard, lounge chair, ottoman, desk chair) and the mirrors and occasional tables that go with them. Typically 14 to 20 SKUs per room type.

What we do is hold that set together: one specification review, one sample round, one inspection calendar, one loading plan. The factories are partners. The accountability for the package is ours.

The Room-Type Matrix

Most budget overruns on a guestroom package are not price increases. They are SKU changes between room types that nobody priced at tender.

Room type What changes SKUs affected Where the cost moves
Standard king Baseline set — —
Twin / double Two beds, two nightstands, wider headboard wall +3 to +4 Upholstery and casegoods volume, not the frame count
Junior suite Adds lounge chair pair, coffee table, larger desk +4 to +6 Upholstery yardage is the main driver
Full suite Adds dining table, sideboard, second wardrobe, occasional seating +10 to +14 Freight class changes: this is where consolidation pays
Accessible Knee clearance at desk and vanity, lower wardrobe rod, reinforced grab points 6 to 8 modified Engineering, not materials: expect a per-unit premium
Connecting Door surround millwork, reduced wardrobe depth on the shared wall 2–3 Site measurement dependency, high rework risk

Contract-Grade Specification: Six Lines That Decide the Room

These are the six specification lines where a residential-grade quote looks cheaper and stops being cheaper by year three.

Line What we specify for contract use Why it matters in a guestroom
Casegoods substrate CARB P2 / TSCA Title VI compliant MDF or plywood, 18mm minimum, sealed on all six faces Unsealed edges swell at the wet wall and around the ice area
Edge treatment 2mm PVC or solid-wood lipping, mechanically bonded, not ironed on Ironed banding lifts in the first year of housekeeping traffic
Drawer runners Undermount soft-close with a rated cycle count, not roller slides A guest pulls a drawer 6 to 10 times a stay; runners are the first failure
Upholstery durability Minimum 30,000 Martindale for lounge seating, 50,000 for contract-heavy zones, with a stain finish Rub count is the only number a brand auditor asks for
Foam High-resilience foam, 35kg/m³ seat core minimum, wrapped, not block Block foam collapses at the front edge within 18 months
Hardware and fixings Corrosion-resistant finish, through-bolted where the item is anchored, anti-tip brackets supplied Coastal and humid properties fail plated hardware first

Where Guestroom Packages Fail

Room-type drift

A twin room was priced as a king with an extra bed. The extra nightstand, wider headboard and different wardrobe depth never made it into the order.

Sample approved against the wrong drawing

The approved finish sits on a sample board, but production runs from an earlier revision. Approval has to name the drawing revision.

Freight class surprise

Suite packages mix casegoods and upholstery in volumes that change the container plan mid-order. Consolidation has to be planned at tender, not at loading.

Accessible rooms treated as standard

Knee clearance and grab-point reinforcement are engineering changes. They cannot be value-engineered out after the drawing is approved.

No packing discipline

Loose cartons instead of marked, room-sequenced crates. The install crew unpacks twice and damages the item once.

Guestroom Package Questions

What is included in a hotel guestroom furniture package?

A room set is normally 14 to 20 SKUs: bed base and headboard, nightstands, wardrobe or closet system, desk and desk chair, lounge chair, ottoman or bench, luggage rack, dresser or media console, mirror, and the occasional table. Door hardware, bathroom fixtures, TVs and safes are OS&E and sit with a different supplier.

Do you quote per room or per project?

Per project, priced line by line against your BOQ, with the room count as the multiplier. A per-room number hides the variance between a standard king and an accessible room, and that variance is usually where budgets move.

Can you match a design intent drawing package?

Yes. We work from the FF package, the spec book and the room-type drawings. Where a drawing cannot be built to the stated tolerance, we say so before sampling rather than after the container lands.

What is the lead time for a guestroom package?

Sample approval runs 7 to 10 days from receipt of drawings and materials. Production for casegoods and upholstery runs 45 to 60 days from approved sample. Add consolidation and sea freight: 25 to 30 days to Jebel Ali, 30 to 40 days to Europe or North America.
