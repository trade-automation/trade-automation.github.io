Quality & Compliance: Who Actually Holds the Certificate

Every FF&E supplier publishes a row of logos. Almost none of them say which legal entity holds the certificate, which product it covers, or when it was last audited. This page does.

The One Sentence That Matters

FoshanFFE does not hold these certificates. We are the integrator. The ISO, FSC, CARB, BIFMA, UL and CE documents belong to the factories, mills and laboratories that make and test the goods. What we control is that the right document exists, covers the item you are buying, is current on the day it ships, and reaches your QA file with the holder's name on it.

That distinction is the reason this page exists. A buyer who is told “we are certified” has learned nothing. A buyer who is handed a BIFMA report addressed to a named factory, for a named model, with a named test date, can defend the decision internally. The second version is the only one worth putting in front of a brand's compliance team.

The Certificate Matrix

Four columns: what the document is, what it actually covers on a hotel package, which party holds it, and how it stays valid. The validity column is a route, not a date, because printing a single expiry date for a document that renews on surveillance audits would be a guess dressed up as evidence.

What This Page Deliberately Does Not Claim

  • No certificate is presented as ours. If a document is listed here and the holder is a factory, the holder is named in the column, not buried in a footnote.
  • No logo wall. A logo without a holder, a model and a date is decoration. We would rather show twelve rows of text that survive an audit.
  • No blanket “we comply with” sentence. Compliance is per item, per market, per material. A single sentence covering a 400-room package is not a statement anyone can verify.
  • No invented expiry dates. Where a document renews on surveillance, we say so and give you the audit route.

How the Evidence Reaches Your QA File

  1. At tender. We list the factories that would run each category and return their certificate set before pricing is agreed, so compliance is a filter and not a surprise.
  2. At order release. Validity is checked against the shipment date. UL listing numbers are verified in the public database rather than trusted from a PDF.
  3. At pre-production. Material substitutions are re-checked. A different laminate, foam or fabric can void a fire report that was correct last month.
  4. At pre-shipment. The document pack is compiled per container and cross-referenced to the packing list, so an inspector can match item to report without calling anyone.
  5. At handover. You receive one index: item, standard, holder, document number, issue date. That index is what a brand audit actually asks for.

Frequently Asked

Does FoshanFFE hold the certificates?

No. We are the integrator. ISO 9001, FSC, CARB P2, BIFMA, UL and CE documents are held by the factories, mills and testing labs that produce and test the goods. Every document we hand over carries the holder's name so your compliance team can verify it at the source.

Can I see the certificate before I place an order?

Yes. Send the scope and the destination market and we return the certificate set for the factories that would run your package, with holder names visible. Full documents are released under NDA where the factory requires it.

What happens if a certificate expires during production?

We check validity at order release and again at pre-shipment. If a certificate lapses mid-production the shipment does not leave until a current document is in the file, or the item is re-sourced. That check sits in the QC schedule; it is not left to the factory to volunteer.

Why is the validity column not a fixed date?

Because most of these documents do not expire on a single date. Management-system certificates run on annual surveillance audits, formaldehyde and fire reports are tied to a specific board or fabric, and declarations of conformity follow the model. We list the renewal route instead of printing a date that would be wrong for your order.
