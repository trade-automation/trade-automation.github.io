Navigating EU Guestroom Automation Standards

The European Union has mandated the implementation of the EN 16798-2:2026 standard for hotel guestroom automation. This regulation specifically targets HVAC controllers, integrated room management platforms, and environmental sensors. The critical barrier to entry is the absolute requirement for third-party testing reports. Products lacking this EN 16798-2 certification will be strictly prohibited from entering the EU market, creating a severe risk of market exclusion.

Compliance Alert: EU FF&E compliance now mandates EN 16798-2:2026 for all guestroom automation systems. Procurement teams must immediately audit existing inventories and initiate emergency third-party testing for non-compliant products to prevent costly project delays and market bans.

Smart Lighting Procurement: Dual Protocols and GCC Access

In the realm of smart lighting procurement, a global shift toward DALI-2 and Bluetooth Mesh dual-protocol solutions is underway. Top global hotel groups are enforcing this dual-protocol mandate, with single-protocol manufacturers facing disqualification from future bids by 2027. This technical evolution requires significant R&D and manufacturing agility.

Simultaneously, the Middle East market is streamlining its technical requirements. The recent expansion of the GCC smart lighting white list now includes eight Chinese DALI-2 gateway manufacturers.
