Project Overview

This mockup room project demonstrates our capability to deliver a complete guestroom FF&E package — every piece of furniture, every finish, fully coordinated and produced under one roof in Foshan.

Before committing to mass production for a hotel rollout, developers and operators require a fully furnished mockup room for approval. This single room involves 14 distinct furniture item types, each requiring precise material selection, finish matching, and dimensional accuracy.

By managing the entire package, we eliminate the risk of mismatched finishes, inconsistent quality across vendors, and fragmented logistics — delivering a turnkey mockup at a transparent factory-direct price.

Material & Craftsmanship Highlights

Every piece is built to hospitality-grade standards with premium materials and finishes that meet international hotel brand requirements.

Ash Solid Wood Frames

White ash (白蜡木) selected for structural frames — known for strength, beautiful grain, and excellent finishing properties. Sourced from certified suppliers.

Oak Veneer Finish

Natural oak veneer applied to casegoods for a premium hospitality aesthetic. Consistent grain matching across all pieces in the same room lot.

Eco-friendly Open-pore Paint

Low-VOC, open-pore finishing allows natural wood texture to show through while providing durable protection. Meets CARB P2 and E1 emission standards.

Metal Reinforcement

Steel reinforcement structures integrated into casegoods for long-term durability in high-traffic hospitality environments. Metal frames combined with plywood core.

Grade-A Fabric Upholstery

Fabric upholstery at 35 CNY/meter grade — selected for hospitality durability, stain resistance, and colorfastness. Wide range of options to match brand standards.

Fire Retardant Options

CAL 117 (US) and BS 7177 (UK/EU) fire retardant treatments available for all upholstered items. Compliance documentation provided with every shipment.

Why the Mockup Room Approach Matters

Building a mockup room before mass production is the smartest risk-mitigation strategy in hotel FF&E procurement.

1

Approve Before You Commit

A fully furnished mockup room lets hotel operators, designers, and owners physically inspect every piece — materials, finishes, dimensions, and craftsmanship — before committing to mass production. This eliminates costly surprises during rollout.

2

One PO Covers Everything

Instead of managing 10+ vendors for different furniture categories, a single purchase order covers all 14 item types in the mockup room. One contact, one timeline, one accountability point — from design validation to delivery.

3

Factory-Direct Transparency

Every item priced at factory-direct rates with full material breakdowns. No hidden markups, no bundled pricing — you see exactly what each piece costs. FOB Foshan terms with full documentation for customs and logistics.
