The Short Answer

What Is the Standard FF&E Lead Time?

A hotel FF&E lead time runs 10–14 weeks from purchase order to delivery for a full package, before ocean transit. Standard production takes 6–8 weeks; items with custom specialty finishes take 14–18 weeks. Ocean freight adds 18–40 days depending on destination. Pre-pandemic the same scope averaged 16–20 weeks.

Most project schedules fail on FF&E not because the factory is slow, but because the package is released late. The production window is the shortest part of the timeline — coordination, sampling and documentation consume as much time again, and all three sit upstream of the factory.

Risk Management

The Five Things That Actually Delay FF&E Deliveries

Four of the five are buyer-side. None of them are factory capacity problems.

  • Late approval of the finish schedule. Every week of delayed sign-off is a week added to the delivery date, because production cannot start without an approved sample.
  • Drawing changes after production release. Post-release changes restart the affected items — including the sampling cycle — rather than pausing them.
  • Incomplete brand-standard specifications. When the brand standard is referenced but not supplied, the item has to be re-quoted mid-project, which resets its position in the queue.
  • Missing compliance documentation. Fire and material certificates that arrive after loading can hold a container at customs longer than the ocean voyage itself.
  • Container consolidation windows. If the last category is not packed before the loading window closes, the whole booking moves to the next vessel rather than shipping partially.

Schedule Recovery

How to Compress an FF&E Schedule

Two to four weeks of recovery is realistically available on most projects, using five levers:

Lever Time Recovered Trade-off
Approve finishes from a pre-tested material library 1–2 weeks Slightly narrower finish range
Release standard items before custom items are signed off 1–2 weeks Two production waves to track instead of one
Split the package into two container waves 2–3 weeks to site Two freight bookings instead of one
Pre-clear compliance documentation before loading 3–5 days at customs Requires certificates earlier from each factory
Use one consolidated supplier 1–2 weeks Requires an integrator rather than five vendors
