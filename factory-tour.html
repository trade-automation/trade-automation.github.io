Inside Our Foshan Factory

A transparent look at the craftsmanship behind every hotel FF&E project

Our Foshan Production Facility

Located in the heart of China's furniture manufacturing capital, our factory has been dedicated to hotel furniture production for over 14 years. Spanning a modern production campus, the facility integrates advanced CNC machinery with time-honored woodworking craftsmanship.

Every piece of hotel FF&E — from guestroom casegoods to lobby statement furniture — is built here under one roof, ensuring quality consistency from raw material to final container load.

We serve hospitality projects across the United States, Middle East, Southeast Asia, and beyond, delivering full guestroom and public area FF&E through a single purchase order.
