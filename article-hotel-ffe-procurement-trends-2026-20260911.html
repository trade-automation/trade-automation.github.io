Mastering EU Guestroom Automation Compliance

European regulatory bodies have introduced the EN 16798-2:2026 standard, establishing a strict framework for hotel guestroom automation. This mandate creates substantial technical barriers for non-compliant suppliers. The regulation strictly requires third-party testing reports for all HVAC controllers and integrated room management platforms deployed within EU member states.

Failure to achieve EN 16798-2:2026 compliance poses severe risks, including the outright ban of non-compliant smart FF&E from the European market. Global hotel brands operating in Europe cannot afford supply chain disruptions caused by customs rejections or failed inspections. Our engineering teams rigorously test all room management systems against these thresholds, ensuring that every piece of FF&E export China meets the precise environmental and operational metrics dictated by Brussels.
