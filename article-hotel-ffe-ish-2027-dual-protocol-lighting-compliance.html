Introduction to the ISH 2027 Smart Lighting Mandate

Global top hotel groups, including major international chains, are enforcing a unified approach to smart lighting compliance. According to industry intelligence, the ISH 2027 standards will officially mandate dual-protocol compatibility for all new hotel lighting procurement projects starting in early 2027. This regulatory shift aims to standardize interoperability across diverse property management systems and guestroom automation platforms.

For hotel developers and procurement directors, this means that single-protocol vendors will be systematically disqualified from future bids. The mandate requires lighting fixtures and control systems to natively support both wired and wireless communication protocols simultaneously. This ensures long-term scalability and reduces the lifecycle costs of guestroom technology upgrades.

Technical Requirements: DALI-2 and Bluetooth Mesh Integration

The core of the new compliance framework revolves around the simultaneous integration of DALI-2 and Bluetooth Mesh technologies. DALI-2 provides robust, wired reliability for critical lighting circuits, while Bluetooth Mesh offers the flexibility needed for wireless sensor networks and mobile guest controls. Achieving true DALI-2 Bluetooth Mesh interoperability requires advanced gateway engineering and rigorous third-party testing.
