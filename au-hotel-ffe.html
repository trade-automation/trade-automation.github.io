Australia-Specific Questions

Under the China-Australia Free Trade Agreement (ChAFTA), wooden furniture originating from China qualifies for 0% import duty. We provide a Certificate of Origin (FORM Y) with every shipment, which your customs broker uses to claim the tariff exemption. Under RCEP, FORM R serves the same purpose.
Yes. All lighting and electrical items are fitted with Australian standard 3-pin plugs (AS/NZS 3112) and wired for 240V/50Hz. Every product carries SAA/RCM compliance marking. We can also supply fixed-wiring luminaires compliant with AS/NZS 60598.
All wood products are fumigated (ISPM 15 standard) and treated before shipping. We provide fumigation certificates and phytosanitary documentation. Processed/engineered wood furniture typically passes Australian Biosecurity inspection without issue — we've shipped hundreds of containers to AU without quarantine holds.
