Breaking Down Each Component

Component #1

FOB Price (Free On Board)

The FOB price includes: raw materials, factory labor, manufacturing overhead, factory profit margin, export packaging, and inland transportation to the Chinese port. This is the price your supplier quotes you.

What's included: Product cost + packaging + trucking to Nansha/Shenzhen port + Chinese export customs clearance

What's NOT included: Everything from the ship's rail onward — ocean freight, insurance, import duties, and final delivery.

2026 range for hotel guestroom set: $4,500-$8,500 depending on specification level, materials, and order volume.

Component #2

Component #4

Customs Brokerage & Port Fees

Your customs broker handles the paperwork to clear your goods through US Customs. Budget for:

  • Brokerage fee: $350-500 per entry (a full hotel FF&E shipment typically needs 1-2 entries)
  • ISF filing: $75-150 (must be filed 24 hours before vessel departure)
  • MPF (Merchandise Processing Fee): 0.3464% of shipment value, min $31.67, max $614.35
  • HMF (Harbor Maintenance Fee): 0.125% of shipment value (ocean shipments only)
  • Terminal handling charges: $200-400 per container at US port
Component #5

Inland Delivery: Port to Hotel Site

Once cleared through customs, the containers need to reach your hotel. Costs vary dramatically by location:

  • Port-adjacent (within 50 miles): $400-600/container
  • Regional (50-300 miles): $600-1,000/container
  • Cross-country (300+ miles): $1,000-1,800/container
  • Urban delivery restrictions: Add $200-500 for cities with truck access/time restrictions (NYC, SF, Miami Beach)

How to Reduce Your Landed Cost

  1. Flat-pack design: Shipping disassembled increases container utilization by 40-60%, reducing per-room freight cost by 15-25%
  2. Consolidated shipping: Full container loads (FCL) vs. shared containers (LCL) save 25-35% on freight
  3. HTS optimization: Working with a knowledgeable customs broker on classification can save 5-8% on duties
  4. Volume negotiation: 200+ room orders typically achieve 10-15% FOB price reduction vs. small orders
  5. Off-peak ordering: Production scheduled for July-August or January can yield 3-5% factory pricing advantage
  6. West Coast + rail: Ship to LA/LB then rail to East Coast can be $500-800/cheaper per container than all-water route
