Hotel FF&E Sourcing FAQ

Everything you need to know about sourcing hotel furniture, fixtures, and equipment from Foshan, China — lead times, certifications, quality control, shipping, and more.

General FF&E Questions

What is hotel FF&E?

FF&E stands for Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment. In the hospitality industry, it refers to all movable furniture, fixed fixtures, and equipment used in hotel guestrooms, lobbies, restaurants, and public areas. Our scope covers full guestroom packages (beds, nightstands, wardrobes, desks), public area furniture (lobby seating, banquet tables), lighting, mattresses, drapery, and bathroom vanities — plus hard finishes like stone, glass, aluminum doors, and bathroom fixtures through our 19-factory partner network in Foshan.

Where is your manufacturing base?

Our manufacturing hub is in Foshan, Guangdong — China's largest furniture production base. We coordinate a network of 19 specialized partner factories covering casegoods, upholstery, metalwork, stone, lighting, mattresses, and bathroom fixtures. This integrated network allows us to deliver complete FF&E packages efficiently.

Sourcing & Production

How long does hotel FF&E sourcing and production take?

Typical lead times: sampling 30-45 days, bulk production 48-60 days after sample approval. Complex projects with custom designs may require 60-90 days. We coordinate production across our partner factory network to meet tight opening deadlines. Lead times are subject to material sample approval upon order confirmation.

Can you supply full guestroom packages on one purchase order?

Yes. We are a hospitality FF&E integrator, not a single-category supplier. We deliver full guestroom + public-area packages on one PO — casegoods, soft seating, lobby furniture, lighting, mattresses, drapery, and bath vanities. One supplier-of-record from drawing to container.

Can you match specific design requirements?

Absolutely. We work from architect drawings, designer specifications, or concept sketches. Our team provides 3D rendering services for design validation. Materials, finishes, and dimensions are confirmed with you before production begins. On-site verification is recommended for final dimensions.

Can you supply both FF&E and hard finishes (stone, glass, doors)?

Yes. While our core strength is FF&E integration, we coordinate hard finishes — stone, glass, aluminum doors/windows, curtain walls, aluminum panels, hardware, and bathroom fixtures — through our partner network. Everything routes through us as your single point of contact.

Quality & Certifications

What certifications do your products carry?

Our products meet international standards including UL (Underwriters Laboratories) for North American market compliance, CARB (California Air Resources Board) for formaldehyde emission standards, ISO 9001 for quality management, and BSCI for social compliance. Specific certifications vary by product category and can be provided upon request.

How do you ensure quality control?

We implement a multi-stage QC process: material inspection upon arrival, in-process quality checks during production, pre-shipment inspection with photo documentation, and final container loading supervision. Third-party inspections (SGS, TÜV, Bureau Veritas) are welcomed and can be arranged at your request.

Do you offer warranty on your products?

We provide a standard 2-year warranty on structural components and workmanship. Specific warranty terms vary by product category and are detailed in the contract. Post-warranty support and spare parts are available.

Orders & Commercial

What is your MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)?

We work on a project basis rather than per-unit MOQs. Whether you need FF&E for a 50-room boutique hotel or a 500-room resort, we coordinate production to match your project scope. Sample orders are welcome for quality evaluation before bulk commitment.

What payment terms do you offer?

Standard terms: 30% deposit upon order confirmation, 70% balance before shipment. For established partners and repeat orders, flexible terms can be negotiated. All transactions are secured through formal contracts with clear specifications and delivery timelines.

Shipping & Logistics

Do you handle shipping and logistics?

We provide EXW (Ex Works) China pricing as standard. We can coordinate with your preferred freight forwarder or recommend trusted partners for FOB, CIF, or DDP shipping. All items can be packed and loaded in the same container to minimize shipping costs.
