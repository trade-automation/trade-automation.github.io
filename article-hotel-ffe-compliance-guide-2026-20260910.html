Navigating EU Guestroom Automation Standard EN 16798-2:2026

The European Union has officially mandated the implementation of the EN 16798-2:2026 standard for hotel guestroom automation systems. This regulation strictly governs the performance and interoperability of HVAC controllers, integrated room management platforms, and environmental sensors. Products lacking comprehensive third-party testing reports will be categorically banned from the EU market, posing a severe risk to projects utilizing non-compliant equipment.

Key Compliance Alert: All hotel room automation systems deployed in EU properties must feature verified third-party testing reports aligned with EN 16798-2:2026. Procurement teams must immediately audit their current smart room suppliers to prevent customs seizures and project delays.

To mitigate these risks, buyers must execute a rigorous vendor audit before finalizing any purchase orders. Obtaining these certifications requires rigorous laboratory testing of both hardware components and software communication protocols. Procurement teams should verify the following:

  • Valid third-party testing reports for all HVAC controllers and environmental sensors.
  • Documented interoperability metrics meeting the precise energy efficiency standards defined in EN 16798-2:2026.
  • Supplier commitments to update firmware and control logic to maintain ongoing compliance.

Dual-Protocol Mandates for Smart Lighting: DALI-2 and Bluetooth Mesh

The specifications for hotel smart lighting are undergoing a fundamental shift. Leading global hotel groups are enforcing new procurement rules for 2027 that mandate dual-protocol compatibility. Specifically, lighting control solutions must seamlessly integrate both DALI-2 and Bluetooth Mesh protocols. This dual-protocol requirement ensures robust, scalable, and flexible lighting infrastructures across diverse property types.

ProtocolPrimary Application2027 Procurement Status
DALI-2Hardwired commercial lighting controlMandatory (Dual-Protocol Required)

This dual-protocol architecture allows facility managers to utilize wired DALI-2 for critical public spaces while deploying wireless Bluetooth Mesh for flexible guestroom configurations and rapid retrofitting. Manufacturers producing single-protocol DALI-2 gateway devices or standalone Bluetooth Mesh lighting will lose their bidding qualifications. Buyers must rigorously verify product specifications to future-proof their investments.
