1. Who we are

foshanFFE.com is a hospitality FF&E (furniture, fixtures and equipment) integrator based in Foshan, Guangdong, China. We supply complete guestroom and public-area furniture packages to hotel owners, developers, procurement agents and interior design firms, primarily in the United States, the Middle East, Europe and Australia.

For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), foshanFFE.com is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and our enquiry channels. Our contact details are in section 13.

This website is a business-to-business (B2B) information site. It is not directed at consumers and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us

When you contact us — by using an enquiry form on this site, sending an email to info@foshanFFE.com, messaging us on WhatsApp, or subscribing to our newsletter — we may collect:

  • Your name and job title
  • Your company or organisation name
  • Your business email address and telephone or WhatsApp number
  • Country and, where relevant, project location
  • Project details you choose to share: property type, key count, budget range, specification documents, drawings or schedules
  • The content of your message and any attachments

Please note that the enquiry forms on this site are mailto forms: when you submit one, your own email application opens and sends the message to us directly. The form data is not stored on our web server.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you browse this site we, and the service providers listed in section 5, may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (typically country or city level)
  • Browser type and version, operating system and device type
  • Pages viewed, time on page, referring page or search query, and outbound link clicks
  • Anonymised identifiers used to recognise repeat visits

We use this information in aggregate to understand which guides and service pages are useful and to keep the site secure and available.

3. How we use it and our legal bases

We use personal data only for the purposes below. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the corresponding legal basis is shown in brackets.

  • Responding to enquiries and preparing quotations — answering your questions, preparing specifications, samples and pricing, and following up on an active project. (Performance of, or steps towards, a contract; legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries.)
  • Maintaining the business relationship — keeping records of projects, orders and correspondence for production, quality control, shipping and after-sales support. (Performance of a contract; legitimate interests.)
  • Sending the newsletter — sharing industry insights, sourcing guides and market updates with people who asked to receive them. (Consent, which you may withdraw at any time.)
  • Understanding site usage — measuring which pages and topics are read so we can improve our content. (Legitimate interests in improving our services.)
  • Security and abuse prevention — protecting the site against bots, spam and malicious traffic. (Legitimate interests.)
  • Legal and accounting obligations — retaining records where the law requires it. (Legal obligation.)

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects, and we do not use enquiry data to train third-party AI models.

4. Cookies and analytics

This site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — analytics cookies (names beginning _ga) set by Google. GA4 records page views, session data and approximate geography. Google Analytics does not log or store full IP addresses for GA4 properties; IP data is used transiently to derive coarse location and is then discarded.
  • Cloudflare — a security cookie (__cf_bm, and related cookies) used to distinguish human visitors from automated traffic and to protect the site from abuse. These are strictly necessary for the operation and security of the site.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which requires your browser to make a request to Google. No cookies are set by the font request itself.

We do not run advertising, retargeting or social-media tracking pixels on this site. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time; blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how parts of the site work. You can also opt out of Google Analytics across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run this website and our business, and only to the extent needed. These providers act on our instructions as processors:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — content delivery, DNS and security (including the security cookies described above).
  • GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) — static hosting of the site's pages.
  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. — Google Analytics 4 measurement and Google Fonts delivery.
  • Intuit Inc. (Mailchimp) — newsletter list management and email delivery for subscribers.
  • Email and messaging providers — the email, hosting and messaging services we use to receive and reply to your enquiries.

We may also disclose personal data where we are legally required to do so, to respond to lawful requests from public authorities, or to protect our rights, property or safety. If our business is ever reorganised or transferred, enquiry and subscriber data may be transferred as part of that transaction; we will notify affected individuals where the law requires it.

Where a project requires it and you ask us to, we may introduce you to a partner factory or a specialist supplier in our network. We will only share your contact details with that partner with your knowledge and for the purpose of progressing your project.

6. International transfers

We are based in China and many of our customers are in the United States, the Middle East and Europe. The service providers listed above operate servers in multiple countries, so your data may be processed outside your country of residence, including in the United States and the European Economic Area.

Where personal data of individuals in the EEA or the UK is transferred outside those regions, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum where relevant), together with the technical and organisational measures our providers maintain. You may request more information about these safeguards using the contact details in section 13.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and quotations that do not lead to an order: up to 24 months from the last contact, so we can pick up a project that resumes later.
  • Active and completed projects: for the duration of the commercial relationship and for as long as needed to support warranties and after-sales service, plus any period required by tax and accounting law.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details.
  • Analytics data: retained in GA4 for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Access — to be told what personal data we hold about you and to receive a copy.
  • Rectification — to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Erasure — to ask us to delete your personal data where we no longer have a lawful reason to keep it.
  • Restriction and objection — to ask us to limit how we use your data, or to object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Portability — to receive data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (for example the newsletter), to withdraw it at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Complain — to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what categories of personal information we collect and how we use it, to request deletion or correction of your personal information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CPRA, and we have not done so in the preceding 12 months.

How to exercise your rights

Email info@foshanFFE.com with the right you wish to exercise and enough detail for us to identify you. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, normally within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

9. Newsletter

You can subscribe to our industry insights newsletter using the form on some of our insight pages. We use Mailchimp to manage the list and deliver the emails. Subscribing requires your email address; providing your name or company is optional.

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us at info@foshanFFE.com to be removed. We will action unsubscribe requests promptly and will not add you to any other list.

10. Security

This site is served over HTTPS with a valid TLS certificate, and the static files are hosted on a managed platform behind a protective CDN. Access to enquiry correspondence and newsletter lists is limited to the team members who need it. We keep security headers (including frame restrictions and a content security policy) in place to reduce common web risks.

No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we review our measures as our systems and legal obligations develop.

11. Children

This website and our services are intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, our service providers or the law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page always shows the current version. Where a change is material and we hold your contact details, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.
