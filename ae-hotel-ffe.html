Zero Additional Tariff

The UAE imposes 5% customs duty on furniture imports — but no additional tariffs, no hidden fees. We handle GCC customs clearance, all documentation, and provide competitive DAP/DDP pricing to your project site.

Civil Defence Compliant

All upholstered and wood products meet UAE Civil Defence fire safety requirements. We provide fire test certificates (BS 5852, BS 7177, NFPA 286) accepted by Dubai and Abu Dhabi authorities.
