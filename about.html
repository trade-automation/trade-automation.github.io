About FoshanFFE

Your Foshan-based integrator — connecting hotel projects with the right factories, certifications, and timelines.

From Foshan to the World

Foshan is China's furniture and building materials capital — home to thousands of factories that produce the world's hotel furniture, tiles, stone, and aluminum products. But navigating this landscape as an international buyer is complex: language barriers, inconsistent quality, fragmented logistics.

FoshanFFE was built to solve this. We are a Foshan-based integrator with a curated network of 19 partner factories — each vetted for export capability, international certifications, and production consistency.

We specialize in hotel FF&E packages: furnishing complete guestrooms and public areas — from casegoods and soft seating to lighting, drapery, mattresses, and bath vanities — all coordinated under a single purchase order. As a complementary line, we also supply building materials: aluminum doors and windows, curtain wall systems, natural stone, tiles, and hardware.

Our clients include hotel groups, hospitality management companies, and interior design firms across the Middle East and North America. We handle everything from drawing review to container loading — so you deal with one team, one PO, and one delivery timeline.

Certified for Your Market

International hotel projects require compliance with strict safety and environmental standards. Our partner factories maintain the certifications your projects demand:

UL

UL Certification

Underwriters Laboratories certification for furniture safety and fire resistance — required for North American hotel projects.

CARB

CARB P2 Compliance

California Air Resources Board Phase 2 formaldehyde emission standards — ensuring low-VOC wood products for guest health.

CE

CE Marking

European Conformity marking — demonstrating compliance with health, safety, and environmental protection standards for international markets.
