About foshanFFE.com

One PO. Full FF&E. From Foshan to your hotel property — anywhere in the world.

Our Mission

We exist to make hotel FF&E procurement simple, transparent, and cost-effective. As a Foshan-based integrator, we coordinate the world's largest furniture manufacturing cluster on behalf of hotel developers, owners, and operators worldwide.

Our model is straightforward: one purchase order covers the entire FF&E package — guestroom furniture, lobby pieces, restaurant fixtures, lighting, bedding, bath vanities, and custom millwork. One invoice. One point of accountability. One team managing the entire supply chain from factory floor to loading dock.

We are not a trading company. We are a specialist FF&E integrator with deep roots in Foshan's manufacturing districts, where specialized factories produce different categories of furniture and building materials. Our role is to source, coordinate, inspect, consolidate, and deliver — all under one integrated program.

The Team

Hospitality FF&E Specialists

The foshanFFE.com team is built around deep expertise in hospitality procurement and Foshan's manufacturing landscape. With years of experience coordinating FF&E programs for international hotel projects, the team understands both the factory floor and the hotel boardroom.

Based in Foshan's furniture district, the team conducts daily factory visits, monitors production quality in real time, and maintains direct relationships with workshop owners and production managers across all major manufacturing sub-districts.

Every project is managed by a dedicated FF&E coordinator who serves as the single point of contact — from initial quotation through sample approval, production, QC, and final delivery.

Certifications & Compliance

We ensure all FF&E meets destination-market fire safety and environmental standards

CAL 117

California flammability standard for upholstered furniture — required for all US hotel projects.

BS 7177

British fire resistance standard for upholstery — mandated across UK and Middle East hospitality projects.

UL / CARB Phase 2

UL listing for electrical components. CARB Phase 2 for formaldehyde emission limits on composite wood products.

ISO 9001

Partner factories maintain ISO 9001 quality management systems for consistent production quality.

BIFMA

Commercial furniture performance testing for safety, durability, and structural adequacy.

FSC CoC

Forest Stewardship Council Chain of Custody certification available for sustainably sourced wood products.
