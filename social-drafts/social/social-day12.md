When metal meets stone, luxury finds its edge.

Copper and marble. Two materials that age with character and command attention in any hospitality setting. In this high-rise hotel project, we integrated both across the lobby, suites, and presidential floor.

The copper hotel table in the lobby lounge develops a living patina that deepens with time, paired with a copper hotel chair featuring a hand-hammered backrest. In the executive suite, the copper hotel desk gleams against a white Carrara top, while a copper hotel cabinet in the minibar area showcases antiqued brass hardware.

Marble brings the counterbalance. The marble hotel sofa's travertine base grounds the seating area in cool elegance, echoed by the marble hotel table's Calacatta gold top in the dining nook. The marble hotel cabinet in the bathroom vanity zone integrates vessel sinks into seamless stone. Even the marble hotel bed uses a Statuario marble-clad headboard panel — backlit for dramatic effect.
