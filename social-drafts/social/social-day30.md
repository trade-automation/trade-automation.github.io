October wrapped — and what a month of projects it's been.

From designer hotel suites in Southeast Asia to timeshare fit-outs across the Caribbean, our Foshan partner network delivered across the full spectrum of hospitality FF&E this month.

Here are the highlights.

A designer hotel suite project in Bangkok showcased our ability to coordinate bespoke casegoods with soft furnishings — a complete designer hotel outdoor terrace package added rooftop daybeds and lounge sets to match the indoor palette. The boutique hotel furniture collection for a 40-room property in Portugal featured hand-finished brass inlays and artisan tile inlays — boutique hotel furnishing at its most curated.

On the volume side, a timeshare FF&E rollout in Mexico required 800+ room packages delivered across four phases. Our timeshare fit-out team managed staggered production and shipping to match construction milestones. Meanwhile, bulk hotel furniture orders for two convention properties — 1,200 rooms combined — and bulk commercial furniture for a corporate hospitality center proved that scale doesn't mean sacrificing detail.
