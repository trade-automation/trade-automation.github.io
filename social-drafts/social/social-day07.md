Three anchor pieces. Infinite design possibilities.

A hotel room's character often comes down to three key furnishings — the sofa, the dresser, and the headboard. Get these right, and the entire room falls into place.

In this boutique hotel project, each piece was custom-engineered. The commercial dresser doubles as a media console with hidden cable management, finished in smoke oak veneer with soft-close drawers. The hotel dresser variation used in the resort wing adds a built-in minibar compartment — a guest favorite.

The hotel headboard spans wall-to-wall in acoustic fabric, integrating reading lights and USB ports. Upstairs in the villas, the resort headboard swaps to hand-woven rattan, while the resort dresser features a marble countertop and brass pulls. The villa dresser takes a minimalist route — solid ash, finger-joint drawers, and a natural oil finish.

And tying every room together: the hotel sofa — specified here in high-performance velvet with a removable, washable cover.
