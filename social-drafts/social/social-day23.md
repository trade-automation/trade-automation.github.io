Shipping hotel furniture internationally means navigating HS codes, tariffs, and weight calculations that can make or break your project budget. Here's what every procurement manager should know.

Most hotel seating — including lobby chairs, dining chairs, and guestroom armchairs — falls under 9401.61 hotel chair classification: wooden-frame seats with upholstery. Hotel casegoods like desks, dressers, and cabinets typically land under HS code 9403 hotel furniture: other wooden furniture for commercial use. Getting these codes right determines your duty rate.

For US importers, the Section 301 tariff furniture surcharge remains a critical cost factor. The current furniture tariff rate 2026 schedule adds significant percentage on top of standard duties.
