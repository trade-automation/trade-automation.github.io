Where the skyline meets serenity.

These images come from a beachfront resort project where the rooftop bar and ground-floor spa had to feel worlds apart — yet belong to the same brand. The challenge? Weather resistance upstairs, moisture resistance downstairs, and luxury throughout.

The rooftop furniture selection centers on a modular rooftop sofa in Sunbrella fabric, paired with a teak-framed rooftop table that seats eight comfortably. Overhead, the rooftop lighting design uses dimmable LED pendants rated IP65, creating a golden-hour glow from sunset to last call.

Downstairs, the spa tells a quieter story. A reception spa desk in honed stone anchors the lobby, while spa seating in the relaxation lounge uses moisture-proof cushions on powder-coated aluminum frames. Every piece of spa furniture was specified for high humidity, easy cleaning, and that spa-grade calm.

From open-air rooftops to steam-filled treatment rooms — we engineer for the environment so the design stays effortless.
