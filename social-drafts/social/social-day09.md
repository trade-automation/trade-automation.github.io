Teak and mahogany have defined luxury hospitality interiors for decades — and for good reason. In this heritage hotel renovation, our team paired FSC-certified Burmese teak with African mahogany to create a warm, layered palette that honors the property's colonial roots.

The teak hotel desk anchors the guest workspace with a live-edge slab top and concealed cable tray. Beside it, the teak hotel sofa frames the seating area in oiled teak slats with weather-resistant cushions. A teak hotel cabinet in the entryway hides luggage storage behind louvered doors, while the teak hotel bed platform floats on recessed bases for a clean, modern silhouette.

Across the hall, mahogany takes the lead. The mahogany hotel desk features a writing slope and leather inset top. The mahogany hotel cabinet showcases book-matched grain panels, and the mahogany hotel sofa in the presidential suite pairs a solid mahogany frame with cream mohair upholstery. Even the mahogany hotel bed uses a carved mahogany headboard with hand-rubbed lacquer.
