Navigating US & Canadian Hotel FF&E Standards

The North American hospitality market demands strict compliance with a layered regulatory framework — federal, state, and local. For hotel operators procuring FF&E from international suppliers, understanding these requirements upfront prevents costly delays, customs holds, and rejection at the property level.

Our Foshan-based factory network maintains active compliance with all major North American standards. Here is what matters for your hotel project:

  • CARB P2 / TSCA Title VI — The California Air Resources Board Phase 2 standard (and the federal TSCA Title VI equivalent) limits formaldehyde emissions from composite wood products (plywood, MDF, particleboard). This is mandatory nationwide for any furniture containing engineered wood. Our factories use CARB P2-compliant adhesives and hold current test reports from SGS, Intertek, or TUV.
  • CAL 117 — California Bureau of Home and Community Services Technical Bulletin 117 sets open-flame and smolder-resistance requirements for upholstered furniture. Any soft seating (lobby sofas, guestroom armchairs, restaurant booths) shipped to North America must pass CAL 117 testing. We coordinate testing at approved labs and provide certification with each shipment.
  • UL Listing — Underwriters Laboratories certification is required for all electrical components in hotel furniture — table lamps, wall sconces, integrated LED fixtures, and USB charging modules. Our lighting partner factory maintains UL listing for hotel-grade luminaires and can provide UL file numbers for your project documentation.
  • ADA Compliance — The Americans with Disabilities Act affects furniture dimensions and clearances in public areas. Reception desks must include a lower accessible section; lobby seating layouts must accommodate wheelchair turning radiuses; restaurant tables require accessible height. We design to ADA guidelines as standard for US projects.
  • Lacey Act — US federal law prohibits import of wood products harvested or traded illegally. We provide chain-of-custody documentation and species identification for all solid wood and veneer components in our furniture.
  • CPSC 16 CFR 1630/1631 — Federal flammability standards for mattresses. All mattresses exported to the US must meet these open-flame resistance requirements and carry a permanent law label with manufacturer identification number.

Our quality control team prepares a complete compliance dossier for each North America shipment — test reports, material declarations, UL file numbers, and Lacey Act documentation — ready for your project files and operator handover.

Trade Terms: We offer FOB Foshan/Nansha/Shenzhen, CIF destination port, or DDP (delivered duty paid) to your project site. FOB gives you maximum control over freight costs; DDP gives you maximum convenience. Most hotel developers prefer CIF or DDP for renovation projects with tight timelines.

Customs & HTS Classification: All hotel furniture is classified under HTS Chapter 94 (Furniture; bedding, mattresses, etc.). Common codes include 9403.60 (wooden furniture), 9401.61 (upholstered seating), 9404.21 (mattresses). We prepare accurate commercial invoices with correct HTS codes to minimize customs examination risk and duty calculation errors.

Tariff Considerations: Most hotel furniture from China enters the US at standard MFN duty rates (typically 0–6% ad valorem). However, certain categories may be subject to Section 301 tariffs. Our team monitors current tariff schedules and can advise on structuring your order to minimize duty exposure. For Canadian projects, furniture enters under most-favored-nation rates with no additional punitive tariffs.

Documentation Package: Every shipment includes commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading (original or telex release), certificate of origin, ISF 10+2 filing data, fumigation certificate, and all required compliance documentation (CARB declarations, UL file numbers, Lacey Act declarations). We provide digital copies at least 5 days before vessel arrival so your customs broker can pre-clear.

Frequently Asked Questions

What certifications do hotel furniture imports need for the US market?

Hotel furniture imported into the United States must meet several certification requirements. All upholstered furniture sold in California must comply with CAL 117 fire safety standards for open flame and smolder resistance. Wood products containing composite wood materials must meet CARB P2 or TSCA Title VI formaldehyde emission limits — this applies nationwide. Electrical components such as bedside lamps and wall sconces require UL listing. Mattresses must meet CPSC flammability standards under 16 CFR 1630/1631. Additionally, products must comply with the Lacey Act regarding legal sourcing of wood materials. Our factory network maintains current UL, CARB P2, and CAL 117 test reports and provides third-party certification documentation for your project files.

How do you handle shipping and customs for North America?

We offer FOB Foshan/Nansha/Shenzhen, CIF destination port, and door-to-door delivery options. Our team also assists with antidumping duty considerations and tariff optimization strategies.

Can you match brand standards for Marriott/Hilton/IHG flag properties?

Yes. We have experience producing FF&E to the specifications of major hotel brand standards including Marriott International, Hilton Worldwide, IHG Hotels & Resorts, Accor, and Hyatt.
