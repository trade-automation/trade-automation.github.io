Understanding US Tariffs on Chinese Furniture

Key factors affecting your landed cost when importing hotel FF&E from China.

Base Duty Rate

Most hotel furniture (9401-9403) carries a base duty rate of 0-3.3% under normal trade relations (NTR). Mattresses and bedding (9404) range from 3-6%. Lighting (9405) is typically 3.9%.

Section 301 Additional Duty

Chinese-origin furniture is subject to Section 301 tariffs. As of 2026, most furniture items carry an additional 25% duty on top of the base rate, bringing total duty to 25-28% for most FF&E categories.

Exclusion Opportunities

Some furniture items have received Section 301 exclusions. Working with an experienced FF&E integrator who understands tariff classification can help identify duty-saving opportunities for your specific product mix.

🌐 Alternative Sourcing

For projects sensitive to tariff costs, consider our China + Vietnam + India sourcing strategy. Certain product categories may qualify for lower duties when manufactured outside China.

📦 De Minimis Threshold

Shipments valued under $800 (per person, per day) entering the US qualify for de minimis treatment and are duty-free. This applies to sample shipments but not bulk project orders.

💡 Classification Strategy

Proper HS code classification can significantly impact duty costs. For example, upholstered vs. non-upholstered seating falls under different subheadings. Always confirm classification with your customs broker before shipment.

Common Questions

Quick answers for importers navigating HS codes and tariffs.

How do I determine the correct HS code for my hotel furniture?

HS code classification depends on: (1) primary material (wood, metal, plastic), (2) function (seating, storage, sleeping), and (3) specific use (bedroom, kitchen, office). Use the table above as a starting point, then confirm with your customs broker based on your exact product specifications.

What is the total duty rate for importing hotel furniture from China to the US?

For most hotel furniture (9401-9403), expect 25-28% total duty = base rate (0-3.3%) + Section 301 additional duty (25%). Textile items (6302-6303) typically have lower total duties of 7-10%.

Can I reduce tariff costs by sourcing from Vietnam or India?

Yes. Vietnam and India-origin furniture is not subject to Section 301 additional duties. For price-sensitive projects, our multi-country sourcing strategy can reduce total landed costs by 15-25%.
