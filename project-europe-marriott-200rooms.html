Project Background

A Marriott-branded hotel in Western Europe was approaching its 10-year renovation milestone. The property owner engaged our team to supply a complete guestroom FF&E package covering 200 rooms — replacing all casegoods, upholstered furniture, lighting fixtures, window treatments, and carpet. The renovation had to be executed in phases, with the hotel remaining partially operational throughout.

Marriott International's brand standards are among the most rigorous in the industry. Every material, finish, dimension, and functional detail must pass a Global Design compliance review before production begins. The procurement team had previously experienced delays with a European supplier who underestimated REACH compliance documentation, pushing the project 4 weeks behind schedule. Our team was selected because of our proven track record with Marriott brand audits and ability to deliver full REACH documentation packages alongside shipment.

The phased installation strategy — working floor by floor while the hotel continued operating — demanded precise delivery scheduling. Each 40HQ container had to arrive at the property within a 48-hour window aligned with the renovation crew's schedule, with items palletized and labeled by room number for direct-to-room delivery.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge 1

Marriott Global Design Brand Audit Compliance

Every furniture dimension, material finish, hardware detail, and functional specification had to exactly match Marriott's Global Design Standards manual — a 200+ page document with brand-specific requirements for bed height, desk clearance, minibar placement, and more.

Solution

Our project team studied the full brand manual before the first drawing was produced. A detailed compliance matrix was built mapping each requirement to our production spec. The mockup room was reviewed by Marriott's regional design director via live video — approved with zero mandatory changes on the first submission.

Challenge 2

EU REACH SVHC Documentation for Every Material

EU REACH regulation requires documentation proving no Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC) above 0.1% threshold in any component — including adhesives, finishes, fabric dyes, and foam. Previous supplier's failure to provide complete documentation caused a 4-week customs delay.

Solution

Collected REACH declarations from all 14 material suppliers (veneer, board, adhesive, lacquer, fabric, foam, metal hardware, LED components). Compiled into a single REACH compliance dossier (47 pages) shipped with every container. Customs clearance at Rotterdam completed in 48 hours — zero documentation queries.

Challenge 3

Phased Renovation — Floor-by-Floor Delivery

The hotel remained partially operational. Containers had to arrive in exact sequence (Floors 3→5→7→4→6→8) with each container's contents palletized and labeled by room number for direct delivery to the renovation floor.

Solution

Developed a container loading plan aligned with the renovation schedule. Each container packed in reverse-unload sequence, with the first room's items at the door. Pallet labels showed floor number, room range, and QR code linking to the room's installation drawing. Zero mis-deliveries across all 6 containers.

Can you pass Marriott brand-standard audits for hotel FF&E?

Yes. Our team has completed Marriott Global Design compliance reviews for multiple properties. We build a detailed compliance matrix mapping every brand requirement to production specs before the mockup is built. First-submission approval rate is over 90%.

What does EU REACH compliance involve for hotel furniture?

REACH requires documentation proving no Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC) exceed 0.1% in any component. We collect declarations from every material supplier and compile a complete compliance dossier shipped with each container for customs clearance.

Can you handle phased delivery for an operating hotel renovation?

Yes. We plan container loading to match your renovation schedule — floor by floor, room by room. Each pallet is labeled with floor number, room range, and QR code for direct-to-room installation.
